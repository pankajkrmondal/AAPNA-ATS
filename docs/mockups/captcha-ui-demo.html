Recommendation: Cloudflare Turnstile. It's free with unlimited requests, invisible for nearly all real users (no puzzles to annoy your HR team), GDPR-friendly, and it layers cleanly on top of the rate limiting you already have — the captcha stops bots before they burn login attempts, and express-rate-limit stays as the safety net behind it.

What the real integration will touch (later, once you pick)

Step 1 · Frontend

Render the widget

Add the provider's script and widget to AdminLogin.jsx and Login.jsx, exactly where the dashed outline sits above. The widget hands back a one-time token; the Sign In button stays disabled until it arrives.

Step 2 · Backend

Verify the token

In auth.controller.js, before checking the password, POST the token to the provider's siteverify endpoint. Invalid or missing token → reject the login as a bot attempt.

Step 3 · Config

Keys & rollout

Two keys from the provider dashboard: a public site key (frontend) and a secret key (backend .env). Free to create; staging and production get separate key pairs.
