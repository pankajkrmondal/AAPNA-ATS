## B. Quick policy questions

**B4. One candidate, two positions — tests.** Since a candidate can now run two open positions at once: do they take the Zeko screening / Evalground test **once, with the result counting for both positions** *(recommended default)*, or once **per position**?

**B5. One candidate, two positions — offer.** If both journeys reach the Offer stage, would you ever issue two offers to one candidate? **Proposal (default):** the first position to reach Offer puts the other on pause, and the recruiter chooses which one proceeds.

**B6. Feedback reminder limit.** You chose a daily reminder to the interviewer until feedback is submitted, with no escalation. If an interviewer never submits, that means emails forever — **proposal (default): stop after 5 reminders**; the candidate card stays flagged "awaiting feedback" in the app so nothing is lost.

**B7. Vendor updates at Offer / Document stages.** You confirmed vendors get status-only updates with no figures or attachments, and never see the offer letter or document emails. At those two stages, should the vendor receive **(a) a bare status line** — e.g., "Offer shared with candidate on 14-Jul" *(recommended default)* — or **(b) nothing at all**?
