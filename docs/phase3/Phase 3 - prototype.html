Schedule interview

Publish 2–3 open slots from the interviewer's Outlook free/busy — the candidate picks one from an emailed link (no login). First-come holds a slot; the interviewer can update published slots at any time to resolve conflicts (Q31). The Teams invite is created automatically on pick.

On save: Outlook invite with Microsoft Teams link → candidate + interviewer from the recruitment mailbox. Reminders (Q7) — candidate 30 min before · interviewer 30 min before · feedback form right after, then one reminder per day until submitted.

Interviewer scorecard — Interview Evaluation Format

What the interviewer sees via the emailed link — no ATS login needed. Candidate, position, round and interviewer are pre-filled from the schedule. Ratings are out of 5 in 0.5 steps. Same card for Technical 1–3 and CEO rounds (Q18); the HR Round has its own card (timings, relocation, notice, CTC, strengths…).

+ Skill 4 / Skill 5 — optional, same rating + remarks

RT reviews this and records the official round outcome — recommendation and decision are both kept. HR is notified on submission.

IQ / Tech Assessment — import Evalground results

Part of the Assessment round: the Evalground CSV (one file — GA + Technical, Q1) is matched by candidate email to candidates currently in this round, filling their IQ (GA) and Technical scores. Duplicate attempts use the latest row; pass mark 50% for both tests drives the Passed/Failed suggestion (Q4). Nothing is written until you confirm.
