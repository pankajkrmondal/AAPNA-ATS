# For people who only receive emails

**For:** hiring managers, MRF approvers, interviewers, and recruiters answering their questions. **Version:** October 2026.

You don't need an ATS account. Everything you do starts from a link in an email from the AAPNA recruitment mailbox. Each link works only for you and only for a limited time. Don't forward it.

---

## Hiring managers

| Email | What to do |
|---|---|
| **New MRF Request** (from a recruiter) | Click the link and fill in the requisition: role, number of openings, mandatory and good-to-have skills, experience, qualification, budget, and the JD. Submit |
| **Approval outcome** | Your MRF was approved or declined ("Declined: New MRF Request"). Once approved, recruiters start screening candidates for it |
| **Missing JD** | The JD couldn't be read or wasn't attached. Use the link to upload it |

The recruiter can see the MRF's status and approval trail at any time.

## MRF approvers

| Email | What to do |
|---|---|
| **New MRF Request — Approval Request** | Read the requisition. Click **Approve** or **Reject** in the email. The page that opens shows the requisition and your choice; add a comment if you like and confirm |
| **Confirmation** | Your decision was recorded |
| **Already decided** | Another approver decided first. **One approval is enough**; nothing more is needed from you |

- Your link is **personal** and records who decided, when, and your comment. It expires after **14 days**; the recruiter can send a fresh one.
- If you open the link after a decision, the page shows who decided and when. This is not an error.
- No reminders are sent once an MRF has been decided.

## Interviewers

| Email | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Interview Scheduled — Panel** (Outlook invite) | Accept it. It has the Teams link, date and time, and candidate's name and position. Reschedules and cancellations come the same way |
| **Interview Reminder — Panel** | A reminder shortly before the interview |
| **Did the (round) with (candidate) take place?** | Sent after the interview if Teams couldn't confirm it. Open the link: **Yes, we met — score now** takes you straight to the scorecard; **No — it didn't happen** records that. A scorecard is only requested for interviews that took place |
| **Please score your round** (scorecard link) | Fill in the scorecard: ratings, comments, your recommendation. **Submit** within **7 days**; the link expires after that |
| **Reminder: your feedback** | Your scorecard is still waiting |
| **Candidate pack** (from a recruiter, sometimes) | A download with the candidate's resume, reports and earlier scorecards, for an external interviewer. Delete it after **30 days**, as the pack asks |
| **Recording link** (sometimes) | A private link to watch an interview recording. It expires after **14 days** and can be withdrawn |

Interviewers are never told whether a candidate is an employee referral. That keeps the assessment unbiased.

## Candidates (for recruiters answering questions)

| Email | Candidate does |
|---|---|
| Welcome / profile registered | Nothing |
| **Complete your profile** | Fills a short form with missing details (experience, CTC, notice period…). Reminders stop once it's done |
| Shortlisted (with the JD) | Reads about the role; the Zeko interview link follows |
| Zeko interview invitation | Takes the AI interview within the window |
| Evalground test | Takes the test by the due date |
| Interview scheduled / reminder / rescheduled / cancelled | Joins the Teams interview |
| Stage outcome (moved on / on hold / not selected) | Nothing |
| **Documents required** | Uploads documents through the secure link (no login). Up to 3 reminders |
| Offer | Sent by HR outside the ATS; the ATS records it |

Candidates never see interview scores, internal notes or referral information.
