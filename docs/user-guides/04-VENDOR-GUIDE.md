# Vendor guide

**For:** placement vendors who submit candidates to AAPNA Infotech. **Version:** October 2026.

You have two screens: **Dashboard**, which shows where your candidates are, and **Upload Candidate**, where you send resumes. You see only the candidates you submitted.

## 1. Signing in
- AAPNA's recruitment team creates your account; you receive your sign-in details by email.
- Sign in with your email (or username) and password. Use **Forgot password?** if needed, or change it any time: your picture (top right) → **Change Password**.

## 2. Uploading candidates
1. Open **Upload Candidate**.
2. Drag resumes onto the box, or click it. Accepted: **.pdf**, **.docx**, or a **.zip** of several resumes.
3. Click **Upload Resumes**. Each file is read automatically; the list below updates live.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Processing | Being read |
| Saved to Database | Accepted as a new candidate |
| Pending Recruiter Review | This person may already be in AAPNA's database. A recruiter will decide; you don't need to do anything |
| Rejected by System | No email address or phone number was found in the resume. Send a resume with contact details |
| Processing Failed | The file couldn't be read. Try again, or send it to your AAPNA contact |

**The 90-day rule.** A candidate you submit is **yours for 90 days**. If another vendor submits the same person in that time, their upload waits for a recruiter and your claim stands. After 90 days, a new submission can take over.

**Make sure each resume has the candidate's own email and phone number.** Without them the upload is rejected, and AAPNA can't contact the candidate.

## 3. Following your candidates
**Dashboard** shows your submissions: totals, how many are shortlisted, in interviews, on hold, rejected or hired, and a list with each candidate's current stage. Hover over the ⓘ next to a number to see what it counts.

You also receive an **email when your candidate's status changes** at each stage (shortlisted, moved on, on hold, rejected, closed). These emails carry the status only: never interview feedback, salary details or documents. At the offer stage you are told there is a milestone; at the documents stage you get no email.

## 4. Questions

| | |
|---|---|
| Can I see other vendors' candidates? | No |
| Can I see interview scores or feedback? | No. Ask your AAPNA contact |
| A candidate I sent shows "Pending Recruiter Review" for days | The recruiter decides on possible duplicates; ask your AAPNA contact |
| I see an error with "ref: …" | Send the message, including the ref, to your AAPNA contact |
